# Summary of `Модули_1_2_новые.md` (colleague's course, reference only)

Line numbers refer to `Модули_1_2_новые.md`. Use this file instead of re-reading the 4,700-line source.

## What it is

- It is an export of an online course (SkillFactory, `MIFIML-1sem+2024`, line 4598) with auto-graded tasks. Each task has a type, a ticked answer and sometimes a comment.
- The audience is ML/data-science students, so most contexts are servers, classifiers and hashes, with few business ones.
- **Module 1** (1–2784): a probability refresher, limit theorems, sample statistics and point estimation. It has a final test worth 6 points (2677).
- **Module 2** (2785–4708): re-covers the same distributions in SciPy/Matplotlib, with PMF/CDF plots and parameter tables, then a test worth 6 points (4562). It has no theory beyond Module 1 except the extra distributions (negative binomial, Laplace, Pareto, Rayleigh).
- Display math is damaged by the conversion: lines starting with `#` are formulas, and `\,` was lost.

## Not covered (we need our own material)

Combinatorics as a topic, conditional probability and the law of total probability, Bayes, independence (used but never defined), joint distributions, covariance and correlation, efficiency/MSE, sampling distributions (χ², t and F are only named at 4260), confidence intervals, hypothesis testing, chi-square tests and regression.

## Notation there and here

The source uses ξ for random variables, M for the mean, D for the variance, the CDF F(x)=P(ξ<x) in Module 1, and Cₙᵏ. We use X, \E, \Var, F(x)=P(X≤x) (the English textbooks and SciPy convention) and \binom{n}{k}. Φ is the standard normal CDF in both.
